1 Who we are and what we do

We are the Public Health Intelligence Team at the London Borough of Tower Hamlets, comprised of one Health Intelligence Lead and two Senior Analysts.

We provide data and analytical support to the Public Health Division of Tower Hamlets. This includes, amongst other things:

  • Providing analytical support for needs assessments, including:
    • Helping project leads scope out their data needs and develop analysis plans
    • Identifying suitable datasets for specific projects
    • Conducting epidemiological analyses and producing visualisations with accompanying commentary
  • Developing data products (e.g. EDITH) to enable colleagues and partners to easily find information and statistics about the local population to inform public health planning
  • Developing and delivering training sessions to help colleagues upskill in data analysis and public health intelligence
